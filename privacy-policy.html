Privacy Policy for Circuit Guesser

Circuit Guesser is developed and published by Ramazan Çağrı Şafak, operating under the indie studio name Basic Man Studio. This privacy policy explains how user data is handled within the app.

1. Information We Collect

Circuit Guesser does not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII). The app itself does not create user accounts, store personal data, or request permissions.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect non-personal and anonymous data such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Advertising ID
  • Approximate location (not exact)
  • App usage and basic interaction data

AdMob data collection follows Google’s Privacy & Terms. AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Child-Friendly Experience (Under Age 13)

Circuit Guesser is designed to be appropriate for audiences of all ages, including users under 13. The app does not collect personal information from children.

We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by ensuring:

  • No personal data is collected
  • No login, registration, or profile system exists
  • No personal analytics or tracking is performed by the app
  • Advertisements are handled according to Google’s families-compliant ad policies

If you believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately and we will remove such information.

4. How We Use Information

The app itself does not use data in any form. Any non-personal data collected by AdMob is solely for:

  • Displaying ads
  • Measuring ad performance

5. Security

Because the app does not store personal data, no sensitive information is kept on any server or device.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will appear on this page.
